# What I Need From You

A quick list to get the gift shop live. The ⭐ items are the only things that
hold up launch — send those first and everything else can follow.

---

### ⭐ Returns
- Do you accept returns? If so, within how many days, and any rules (unworn, tags on)?
- Where should returns be sent?

### ⭐ Shipping
- Who packs and ships the orders, and what address do they ship from?
- What should shipping cost? (e.g. flat $6, or free over $50)
- US only, or international too?
- Is "free pickup at your venues" real? If so, which venues?

### ⭐ Sales tax
- Just DC, or other states too?

---

### Your products
For each item you want to sell:
- Name and price
- Sizes, and how many of each you have in stock
- A short description (or I can write one)
- Which venue it's for
- Photos

### Your details
- Business name and address
- Support email (where customer questions should go)
- Phone number (optional)
- Social links for the footer

### Links & extras
- Where should the top menu links (Shows, Venues, About) go?
- Where should newsletter signups be sent?
- Want a custom web address like **shop.unionstagepresents.com**?
